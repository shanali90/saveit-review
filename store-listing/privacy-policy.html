Privacy Policy for SaveIt

Last updated: July 15, 2026

SaveIt helps you save links from other apps, organize them, and receive reminders to revisit them. This policy explains what information SaveIt collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Google Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Google, SaveIt receives basic account information needed to identify your account, such as your email address and name. SaveIt does not receive your Google password.

Saved content: When you save a link, SaveIt may store the URL, title, platform/category, save reason, importance flag, reminder-related timestamps, saved timestamp, and completion status. SaveIt may also store generated summaries or metadata used to organize your saved items.

Notifications: If you enable reminders, SaveIt requests notification permission from your device so it can send reminders about unwatched saved items. Notification settings and reminder times may be stored with your SaveIt settings.

Demo mode: If you use demo mode without signing in, saved items and settings are stored locally on your device under demo-only storage keys.

How We Use Information

SaveIt uses your information to provide core app features: saving links, organizing your library, syncing your account data, displaying reminders, and helping you return to saved content.

Data Storage

For signed-in users, SaveIt stores app data in Supabase. Stored data can include saved URLs, titles, categories, save reasons, timestamps, reminder settings, and related metadata. Supabase is used as the app's backend database and authentication provider.

For demo users, SaveIt stores data locally on your device. Demo data is separate from signed-in account data.

Data Sharing

SaveIt does not sell your personal data. We do not share your saved content with third parties for advertising. Data may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the app, such as Google Sign-In and Supabase.

Your Choices

You can use demo mode without Google Sign-In. You can disable notifications in your device settings or in SaveIt settings. You can clear saved data from within the app.

Data Security

SaveIt uses account-based access controls so signed-in users can access only their own saved rows. No system can guarantee perfect security, but SaveIt is designed to limit access to your account data.

Children's Privacy

SaveIt is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the date at the top of this page.
