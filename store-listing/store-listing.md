# SaveIt Play Store Listing

## App Title
SaveIt: Watch Later

## Short Description
Save links from any app and get reminded to watch them.

## Full Description
SaveIt turns your endless stream of "I'll watch this later" links into a clean, organized reminder list.

Share a YouTube video, Instagram reel, TikTok, article, or any web link to SaveIt from almost any platform. SaveIt captures the link, helps organize it with AI-powered metadata, and keeps your library focused on what you actually meant to revisit.

Instead of letting saved videos disappear into bookmarks, chats, and app-specific folders, SaveIt gives every save a simple next step:

- Save from any platform
- Add why you saved it
- Let AI organize the title, category, and context
- Get daily reminders for unwatched saves
- Mark items done when you finally watch them

Use SaveIt for tutorials, recipes, study material, product reviews, creator recommendations, inspiration, and anything else you do not want to lose in the scroll.

SaveIt also includes a demo mode for trying the app locally before signing in, plus Google Sign-In for syncing your saved library with Supabase-backed storage.

Save it. Remember it. Watch it.
